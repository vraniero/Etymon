## Description (4000 max)

"Notte", "night" and "nocturnal" are one word, 6,000 years old. "Padre" and "paternalistic" meet in Latin "pater". Etymon shows you the family tree hidden in the words around you.

SEE THE WORDS AROUND YOU
Point the camera at a menu, a sign or a book page, or take a photo, and Etymon reads the words and draws their network right over them: which ones share a root, which ones mean the same thing in another language. In AR, the network floats in the room.

TALK, AND WATCH THE WORDS CONNECT
Turn on Talk and have a conversation. Every word said grows its tree of ancestors, in the colour of whoever said it. Etymon works out who is speaking and which language each of you uses, and connects your words where they meet: Italian and English, German and Polish, in the same conversation.

TYPE ANYTHING
Type or paste words and read the story of how they're related, follow a word's journey across the map from where it was born, or explore the whole tree in 3D.

PLAY WITH WORDS
• Root Chain: each word must share an ancestor with the one before. Play against the app or with up to six friends.
• Impostor: three words share a root, one only looks like them. Find it.
• Cousin Hunt and Scavenger: name, or find around you, words descended from an ancient root.
• Birthplace: where was this word born?

20 LANGUAGES
English, Italian, Spanish, French, German, Russian, Ukrainian, Belarusian, Bulgarian, Macedonian, Serbo-Croatian, Polish, Czech, Slovak, Slovene, Romanian, Hungarian, Estonian, Lithuanian and Latvian, and every ancestor behind them: Latin, Ancient Greek, Proto-Germanic, Proto-Slavic, Proto-Indo-European and hundreds more. Each language downloads when you first need it.

EVERYTHING ON YOUR IPHONE
The dictionary, the camera reading and the speech recognition all run on your iPhone. No account, no ads, no tracking, and it works offline.

## Review notes (for Apple)

No sign-in is needed, and nothing is sent to a server: the dictionary, text and speech recognition all run on the device. On first launch the app sets up its dictionary from its asset packs (the core comes with the app; each language is an on-demand pack, offered when the app first reads that language, or in Settings → Languages). To try it quickly: Home → Type, keep the sample sentence and tap Connect. Talk needs the microphone: speak in English or Italian (others are detected and added). AR needs a device with a camera.
