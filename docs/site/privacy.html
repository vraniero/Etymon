Privacy Policy

Etymon · Last updated 17 November 2026

In short: Etymon collects no personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and we have no server.

What stays on your iPhone

The words you type, read or say, the scenes and conversations you save, and your settings are kept only on your iPhone. They are never sent to us.

Word lookups use the dictionary on your iPhone. Its languages are downloaded from the App Store, like the app itself.

Camera and photos

When you point the camera at text or choose a photo, the words are recognized on your iPhone. A picture is kept only if you save the scene, and only on your iPhone.

Microphone and speech

In Talk, what people say is turned into words on your iPhone, with Apple's on-device speech recognition. No audio is recorded, stored or sent anywhere. To tell speakers apart, the app compares how voices sound during the conversation; nothing about a voice is kept after you stop listening.

Location

When you save a scene, the app can note where you were, so you can find it on the map later. The place is kept only on your iPhone, with the scene.

Children

The app collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
